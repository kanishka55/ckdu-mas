## 5. Rules that bite you specifically

- `data/raw/` is read-only for values. Blank columns may be filled; existing numbers may not change.
- Any synthetic row carries `source="synthetic"`. **Never mix real and synthetic silently.**
- Fixed random seeds for anything generated.
- `src/core/` is pure functions — **no SPADE imports**. Agent glue goes in `src/agents/`.
- No individual patient data, ever. Published aggregates only.
